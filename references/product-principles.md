# Sazan product principles

Use these criteria when evaluating or building a public skill, agent, application, or monetizable feature.

## Brand boundary

- Public technology products use the **Sazan** brand.
- Do not present them as Zandi Service Center products.
- Keep legal identity, repository ownership, and product branding distinct when needed.

## Opportunity gate

Before building, identify:

- the real present or emerging user pain;
- how users solve it today and why current options are weak, expensive, complicated, or untrustworthy;
- evidence of demand and willingness to pay;
- competitors and meaningful differentiation;
- technical feasibility, operating cost, privacy, security, and defensibility;
- a realistic recurring-revenue or paid-service path;
- why the timing is appropriate.

Being novel or first is not sufficient. Prefer a focused, high-quality core over many premature features.

## Quality and revenue

- Do not market claims that lack measurable evidence.
- No feature ships without relevant verification.
- Keep useful basics fairly priced; charge separately for genuinely costly cloud processing when appropriate.
- Consider subscriptions, usage credits, APIs, business plans, installation, customization, training, and support only when they fit the product's real value.
- Expand from a focused product into an engine, agent, or platform only after the foundation is strong.

## Distribution

Plan discoverability as part of the product: clear public documentation, searchable terminology, examples, releases, a credible landing page, directories, and coordinated social accounts. Account creation, credential handling, public posting, paid promotion, and other external actions still require the user's authorization at the applicable step.
